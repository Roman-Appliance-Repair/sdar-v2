**Injection logic (brace-balanced parsing):**
- Detect `"@type": "LocalBusiness"` (or HomeAndConstructionBusiness) и walk
  backwards to enclosing `{`, then forwards to matching `}`.
- Insert missing fields just before closing `}` с правильной indent
  detection и quote-style matching.
- Idempotent: `has_field()` check перед каждой injection prevents duplicate.
- String/escape-aware parser handles nested objects + comments correctly.
