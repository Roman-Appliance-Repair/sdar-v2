## Zero-invention (OEM + 2+)
- Universal fault chain: filter (6mo) → supply/kink → frozen fill tube (thaw) → inlet
  valve (TECH) → module (TECH) → freezer temp → ice maker off/feeler-arm → reset. Safe DIY
  vs tech split confirmed.
- **Frigidaire:** dual-solenoid inlet valve ("water but no ice" = ice-side solenoid); two
  **CPSC recalls** (2022 feeler-arm ~370k; Feb-2024 slim ice bucket ~400k, free replacement).
- **Whirlpool:** modular crescent-mold (mold heater/thermostat/motor) vs in-door optical IR;
  OEM reset per type. Builds many Kenmore-106.
- **LG:** freezer below 19°F (rec -4°F); Ice On/Off 3-5s; Test button (pinhole) 3s; reset
  (shake/off 30s/wait 4-6h); Craft Ice unplug 10min+24h.
- **GE:** feeler arm = leading cause; reset (side switch 15s / recessed button / arm cycle).
- **Kenmore fridge prefixes:** 106/110/596/562=Whirlpool, 253/417=Frigidaire, 795/796=LG,
  363/364=GE, 401/402=Samsung (soft) — table in answer-first lead + hub cross-links.
- **Samsung:** Bianchi v. Samsung dismissed with prejudice (no nationwide settlement, no
  claim form) — no "file a claim" language (reused from I-2/data-sheet Layer H).
